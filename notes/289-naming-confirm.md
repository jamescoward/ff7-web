# #289: "Use default name" on Cloud's naming screen

## Part 1: semantic layer and Tier 2 (this PR)

**Evidence from before this issue** (#120 budget 4, PR #197, tier2-eval 45823175785):
- md1_1's gate scene opens Cloud's naming screen at route frame 929 of a scene-driving route.
- The screen runs as gameState 5 with `fieldEventCmd` (0x8009ABF5, `g_FieldState.eventCmd`) at 6. `menuOverlay` doesn't change, because the screen loads its own overlay (ff7-decomp, reference only).
- Start, then Circle (each held 4 frames, one group every 30) closed it after 179 frames. Circle alone never did.

**Added:**
- `semantic/controls.js`:
  - `NAMING_ACTIONS.useDefault`: the sequence, hold 4, every 30, and timeout 1800. The timeout is an agent default, about ten times the measured 179 frames.
  - `namingPressAt(f)`: the pure schedule. The route step (`NAME_HOLD`, `NAME_EVERY`) follows the same one, and a test keeps the two equal.
- `semantic/layer.js`, `naming()`: `{ value: boolean, confirmed }`.
  - True only for gameState 5 with fieldEventCmd 6.
  - Out of the menu it doesn't read fieldEventCmd, and its false is as confirmed as gameState.
- `semantic/addresses.js`: `fieldEventCmd` is now **confirmed**, for value 6 only, by `touch-s2n-naming-confirm`.
- `harness/scenarios/touch-s2n-naming-confirm.json`, generated by `runner/test/unit/naming-scenarios.js`:
  - It boots from `touch-md1-1` and routes to gateway 1 with scene driving, using `NAMING_ACTIONS.useDefault` as the `naming` plan.
  - End checks: md1_2, field mode, and fieldEventCmd no longer 6.
  - Metrics: `out_g`, `named`, `nleft`, `smode` and `evt` (expected 5 and 6 on the screen), plus the three checks.

**Not yet verified:** the Tier 2 result, and its determinism over two runs. This PR's Handoff records both.
