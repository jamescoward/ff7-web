Status (2026-11-20): attempt 1 passed (`089f5a6`, tier2 94 pass, 0 fail). `dialogState1` is confirmed; the acceptance scenarios are renamed `save-boot-19a/b` (17 was taken by #216's equip scenarios) and the diagnostic is removed. Attempts used: 1 of 3.

## Why
`dialogue()` (#108) read only window 0 (`dialogState`). #168's last diagnostic (`d731bc3`, `save-boot-16-mds7st1-reach-down`, one run) found a message in **window 1** with window 0 closed: from `harness/saves/disc1-mds7st1.json` (mds7st1, field 144), hold Down 300 frames, Circle 4 frames, 30 frames, and window 1 read 6. A message in window 1 is invisible to `dialogue()`, so the `dialogue-taps` Labs flag can't advance it.

This issue does not chase a choice window; that is #168's.
